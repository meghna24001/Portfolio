Finance Dashboard

A personal finance app that brings monthly cash flow, budgets, and transactions together in one place.

Know more about this project

The problem

Income, spending, budgets, and account activity can be difficult to follow across separate records. This dashboard gives users one place to review their finances and manage transactions.

What I built

  • Monthly income, spending, savings-rate, and net-worth overview
  • Budget tracking and category spending charts
  • Account management, transaction history, and income/expense entry
  • Bank statement imports

A technical challenge

Bank statement exports use different column names and transaction conventions. I built format-independent parsing with header aliases and explicit errors, so supported PDF and spreadsheet exports can be normalized into consistent transaction records.
